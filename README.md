## 🌐 Deploying to Vercel

1. **Upload to GitHub:**
   - Log in to [GitHub](https://github.com/).
   - Click **New repository** (e.g., `bhavya-portfolio`).
   - Select **Public** and click **Create repository**.
   - Click **Add file** -> **Upload files**.
   - Drag & drop or select all files: `index.html`, `style.css`, `script.js`, `profile.jpg`, `vercel.json`, and `README.md`.
   - Click **Commit changes**.

2. **Deploy on Vercel:**
   - Go to [Vercel](https://vercel.com/) and sign in with GitHub.
   - Click **Add New** -> **Project**.
   - Import your `bhavya-portfolio` repository.
   - Leave default settings unchanged and click **Deploy**.
   - Once deployment completes, your site will be live at `https://your-portfolio.vercel.app`!
